# Evonik Competitive Strategy Query

Evonik is facing increasing pressure on its European specialty chemicals business from high
energy costs, lower-cost global competitors, supply-chain disruptions, and difficulties adopting
AI to improve internal processes.

Which strategic investments should Evonik prioritize to remain competitive and achieve
sustainable growth in Europe?

Please evaluate the most relevant factors across all PESTEL dimensions:

- EU industrial policy, subsidies, political stability, and upcoming regulations
- Energy costs across EU member states, inflation, interest rates, and global chemical prices
- Demographic trends, availability of skilled labor, and changing sustainability expectations
- AI adoption, process automation, and other technologies that could improve productivity
- Renewable energy, circular production, and chemical recycling opportunities
- Emissions requirements, safety legislation, and other regulations affecting the chemical sector

The final report should identify concrete growth and efficiency opportunities within the
European chemical sector and propose a prioritized strategic investment roadmap. For each
recommendation, explain its expected competitive impact, key opportunities, major risks,
dependencies, and whether it should be pursued in the short, medium, or long term.
