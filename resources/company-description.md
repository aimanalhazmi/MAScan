# Velocraft GmbH

## Company Overview

- **Name:** Velocraft GmbH
- **Industry:** Bicycle Manufacturing & Cycling Apparel
- **Headquarters:** Berlin, Germany
- **Founded:** 2018
- **Employees:** 50
- **Annual Revenue:** €8 million
- **Business Model:** Direct-to-consumer (D2C) and specialty bicycle retailers
- **Market Focus:** Germany and neighboring EU countries

## Product Portfolio

### City Bikes
- Commuter bicycles

### Race Bikes
- Road bikes
- Gravel bikes

### Bike Clothing
- Jerseys
- Cycling shorts
- Jackets
- Gloves
- Accessories

## Customers

### Primary Customer Segments

1. Urban commuters (25–55 years)
2. Recreational cyclists
3. Amateur racing cyclists
4. Environmentally conscious consumers

### Revenue Share by Geographic Markets

- Germany: 70%
- Austria: 15%
- Netherlands: 10%
- Other EU Countries: 5%

## Operations

### Manufacturing

- Final assembly in Germany
- Frames sourced from suppliers in China and Portugal
- Apparel produced by contract manufacturers in Eastern Europe

### Revenue Share by Distribution Channels

- Online Store: 60%
- Bicycle Retailers: 30%
- Corporate Fleet Sales: 10%

## Financial Situation

### Revenue Share by Product Line

- City Bikes: 45%
- Race Bikes: 35%
- Bike Clothing: 20%

### Financial Metrics

- Annual revenue growth: ~12% over the last three years
- EBITDA margin: 8%
- Moderate debt level
- Profitable since 2021
